## Interests

### A polymath, but with a systems bias.

My interests look broad from the outside. The common thread is simpler:

> I like understanding complex systems, finding their primitives, and rebuilding them into tools I can experiment with.

### 1. Markets + Quant Research

Backtesting, technical indicators, scanners, market-data pipelines, relative strength, pattern discovery, and the infrastructure behind real-time analysis.

### 2. Agentic AI + Workflow Systems

Agents that execute real work: documents, RFPs, databases, ERP workflows, retrieval systems, and durable multi-step automation.

### 3. Systems + Performance

Rust, Go, event-driven architecture, queues, storage engines, networking, and the engineering required to make software reliable under load.

### 4. Cryptography + Decentralized Systems

Solana programs, MPC validator networks, threshold systems, and experiments around trust, coordination, and digital ownership.

### 5. Knowledge Tools

Personal search, local knowledge bases, semantic retrieval, CLI tools, and interfaces that compress the distance between thought and action.

### 6. Creative Computing

Visual experiments, 3D art, interfaces, games, and the overlap between engineering and aesthetic systems.

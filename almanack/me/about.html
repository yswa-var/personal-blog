03 / me?

My work sits between disciplines.

Professionally, I work around backend engineering, agentic AI and workflow orchestration. I studied Computer Science at KIIT University.

Outside the job description, I keep moving into adjacent fields: quantitative finance, distributed systems, cryptography, developer tools, local AI, graphics and creative computation.

That breadth is deliberate. Each field gives me another model for thinking: markets teach uncertainty, distributed systems teach failure, cryptography teaches trust, agents teach orchestration, and art teaches composition.

Current direction: building deeper systems at the intersection of real-time market infrastructure, quantitative research and intelligent software.
